Geocaching Map – Custom Tile Layers

Use any XYZ, TMS, WMS or WMTS map as a background on the geocaching.com maps – see every cache’s favourite points and D/T right on the map, build routes for your GPS, plus place search, coordinate and measuring tools and 161 m circles.

What it does

Small additions to the map you already use, without leaving geocaching.com.

Any map service

XYZ, TMS, WMS and WMTS layers on both /map and /play/map.

Routes for your GPS

Pick caches, pins, addresses and places from a list or right on the map, preview the route and download it as GPX.

Cache list

The geocaches on the map in a list that follows the map – point, click and zoom straight to the cache (/map).

Favourite points

A heart and the number above every cache on the map – spot the best caches at a glance.

D/T on the map

Difficulty and terrain below every cache – see what suits you without opening the cache pages.

FTF

First to find

A red FTF badge on caches nobody has found yet.

Layer editor

URL auto-detect, capabilities browser, live preview, and import/export of your layers.

Place search

Jump to a place or address straight from the map.

Coordinate tools

Read and work with coordinates directly on the map.

Pins and GPX

Mark parking, a trailhead, a solved final or a projected waypoint, give them names and send them to your GPS as GPX.

Measuring

Measure distances on the map.

161 m circles

Show the 0.1-mile proximity circles to see where new caches can be placed.

Install

Takes about a minute.

  1. Install a userscript manager Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey, available for Chrome, Firefox, Edge and Safari.
  2. Click “Install script” Your userscript manager opens and asks you to confirm the installation.
  3. Open the geocaching.com map The new layers appear in the map’s layer picker. Updates are installed automatically.

Questions

 

Does it need a Premium membership?

No, it works on the map for both Basic and Premium members.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Settings are stored locally in your browser. Map tiles are loaded directly from the tile services you select.

Something broke after a geocaching.com update

Please report it on GitHub with your browser, userscript manager and which map page you were using.
